2. **Coverage is partial.** 139 of 373 families have at least one formalized manuscript (37.27%). The remaining families have no Lean source in the official catalogue.
3. **The 208 catalogued declarations are internally linked.** Files, declarations, comparator configs and theorem-name lists all match, with zero `sorry`/`admit`/`axiom`/`Abort` tokens in the declaration files.
4. **Comparator inventory is larger than the catalogue.** 419 configs (418 top-level + 1 nested) exist but only 192 are named by `status.main_results`; 227 are not catalogued there. All 419 solution and challenge modules exist; 357 theorem sets are textually found in the declared solution module, while 62 are hidden by aggregation/imports. This is an inventory gap, not proof that the extra configs are wrong.
5. **Four manuscript stems fall outside the 373-family map, and three are retractions.** Three are withdrawal notices dated 2026-10-06: Kuga–Satake correspondences, Weil classes on split abelian eightfolds, and the rational Hodge conjecture for products of K3 surfaces. The remaining stem, *Sharp integral fillings in CAT(0) spaces*, is an active unlinked manuscript. The root notice is the Weil-classes paper; the other two notices cite its sign error in the stabilization-trace argument. Each notice says the withdrawal concerns the proof, not the truth of the statement.
6. **The local Math 3.0 seal chain is intact but misaligned.** All 373 labels 001–373 pass their report→certificate→Lean cryptographic chain, but the catalogue is indexed 1–378 with gaps 45, 61, 70, 123 and 163. Five local labels are orphaned; families 374–378 have no local seal; only 368 labels align. The underlying coordinates are also generated by a deterministic formula, not extracted from the manuscripts.
7. **Verification debt is concentrated.** 234 families have no formalized manuscript. The largest unformalized clusters include family 34 (60 candidate matches), family 260 (60) and family 376 (60). The full ranked list is in the JSON ledger.
8. **The 721 vs 722 discrepancy is most economically explained by one active orphan stem.** history.md records 300/719 = 42% top-line formalization and two new manuscripts, so 719 + 2 = 721. The catalogue links 718 unique active stems; adding the 3 withdrawn notices gives 721. The remaining stem is *Sharp-integral-fillings-in-CAT(0)-spaces-September-23-2026*, the single active orphan and the +1 to 722. Independently, `CONTENTS.md` has 720 manuscript PDF links plus 1 overview link = 721 PDF links, so 721 is also a PDF-link count. ## Scope and limitations

- The official `formalization.yaml` sets `review.status: unchecked`.
- The declaration audit checks text presence, not Lean kernel acceptance.
- Unreferenced comparator configs may cover definitions or alternative formalizations; they are reported as uncatalogued, not invalid.
- Withdrawal status is transcribed from README notices; the stated sign error is not independently re-verified here.
- The local 373-family seals are integrity seals, not proofs of the manuscripts.
- No Beal, Euler, Navier–Stokes, or other open problem is proved here.
