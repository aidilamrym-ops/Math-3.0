Method and scope

Version stem. Directory name with the trailing -Month-D-YYYY suffix removed. This groups releases of the same manuscript.

Family map. The 373 family records in reports/math30_real_empirical_parameters.json provide the preprint directories. Stems are matched to those directories.

Catalogue reconciliation. CONTENTS.md links are parsed directly. The README's 721 claim is compared against manuscript links, overview links, linked stems and unlinked disk stems. Withdrawal status is read from unlinked manuscript READMEs.

Formalization map. The 176 sources in staging/openai_math/lean/formalization.yaml are mapped by source-directory stem to families.

Declaration audit. Each of the 208 status.main_results entries is checked for file existence, terminal or full dotted declaration presence after comment stripping, comparator existence, and theorem-name membership in the comparator JSON. Placeholder tokens are counted after comment stripping.

Local seal chain. For labels 001–373, the report SHA-256, certificate SHA-256 and the two Lean string constants are recomputed over LF-normalised bytes. The catalogue is indexed 1–378 with five gaps, so only 368 labels align with a catalogue family; 5 labels are orphaned and 5 families are unsealed.

What this is not. This is not a Lean build, not a kernel check, and not a proof of any manuscript. The official catalogue itself marks review status as unchecked.
